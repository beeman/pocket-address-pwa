Pocket Address

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Pocket Address does not collect, sell, or share personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no server of its own.

When you connect, your wallet gives Pocket Address your public Solana address. The app keeps that address in your browser's storage so it can show it again next time, and deletes it when you tap Disconnect. It never receives your private keys and never asks you to sign anything.

Connecting with Seeker Connect routes the encrypted wallet session through Solana Mobile's relay. The relay carries ciphertext only and is subject to Solana Mobile's terms.

Your address only leaves the app when you choose to: when someone scans the QR code, when you tap Copy, or when you tap Share and pick where to send it.

Your wallet app, the Solana dApp Store, your browser, and GitHub Pages may process information under their own policies when you use their services.

Questions about this policy can be raised on the pocket-address-pwa GitHub repository.
